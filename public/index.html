How Sviten plays
  1. Each player gets 5 cards. Betting round, pot limit.
  2. Flop (3 cards). Betting round.
  3. Draw: swap any number of your 5 cards, or stand pat. The big blind draws first, the button last. Draw exactly one and the new card is dealt face up: keep it, or reject it and take a face-down card you must keep. A kept face-up card stays visible to your opponent.
  4. Turn, betting. River, betting.
  5. Showdown splits the pot. Half to the best 5-card hand you hold (no board). Half to the best Omaha hand: exactly 2 of your cards plus 3 from the board.
